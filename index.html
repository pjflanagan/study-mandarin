學習工具

  • Flashcards - A collection of sets I've curated and a few I've found online from other students. Some of these flashcards are also available in PDF form and on a Chrome Extension.
  • 唱歌 - Follow along with song lyrics in Chinese with toggleable pinyin and hover-to-reveal English translations. See more on Google Docs or listen to the Spotify playlist.
  • 注音 Trainer - Randomly pick a character and display only the zhuyin to practice reading. You can also study up with the zhuyin chart.
  • 讀 Tool - Type or copy/paste text to see pinyin and zhuyin, reformats to traditional.

Hi, I'm Peter and I've been studying mandarin for a few years now. Every now and again I find it helpful to build a little web tool to help me practice something. These tools are free for anyone to use.

* These tools were built by a student, not a native speaker. There may be errors. Characters often default to the most common pronunciation which may not be correct in all contexts.
